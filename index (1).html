Straight up I-45 and US-287 through the Piney Woods and out onto the High Plains. Cadillac Ranch sits just off I-40 west of town if there's daylight left — bring a spray can.

While you're in Amarillo

  • Cadillac Ranch — free, open 24/7, best late afternoon light
  • Palo Duro Canyon State Park, ~25 min south of town
  • Big Texan Steak Ranch — the 72oz steak challenge, right on I-40
  • Historic Route 66 district downtown for a walk after dinner

The shortest driving day of the trip — north on US-287 into Colorado, climbing from the Panhandle onto the Front Range. Garden of the Gods is free and a five-minute detour off the highway.

While you're in Colorado Springs

  • Garden of the Gods — free, walk or drive through the rock formations
  • Pikes Peak — summit by car or Cog Railway, weather permitting
  • Manitou Springs for dinner — walkable, mineral springs, mountain-town feel
  • Cave of the Winds or Seven Falls for an indoor/outdoor option

The drive — I-80 past the Bonneville Salt Flats

Straight across Nevada on I-80 — the Bonneville Salt Flats, then a whole lot of Great Basin, before climbing into the Sierra near Reno for the final push over to the lake.

While you're in South Lake Tahoe

  • Heavenly or Sierra-at-Tahoe if you want to ski or ride a day
  • Emerald Bay overlook on the west shore — short drive, big view

The drive — I-8 and I-10 through the Sonoran Desert and West Texas
